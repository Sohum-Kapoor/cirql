Cirql privacy

Last updated Sep 27. Cirql is a personal contacts app you fill from your own notes. This page says what the app stores, what leaves it, and what you can do about it.

What is stored

  • Your account: a username and email, and a password hash.
  • What you type or say: notes and voice memos, the people, facts, wants and promises extracted from them, your goals, your card, and the contact details you add by hand.
  • Every extracted claim keeps a pointer to the words it came from, so you can always see why the app believes something.

Each account's data is isolated by construction: it lives under your own account and the code that runs for another user cannot reach into it.

What leaves the app

  • Note text, your about-me text and goal text go to Google (Gemini) to extract people and rank them for a goal. Audio goes to a speech-to-text provider (ElevenLabs) when you use voice capture. Web enrichment sends a person's name and the context you captured to Google Search grounding, and attaches a fact only with its source URL.
  • Between accounts, only a card you wrote crosses over, granted read-only to one specific person you chose. A network request carries a need, one line about you and your handle, never a name from your contacts. Replies carry blinded identity tokens (hashes), never names.
  • Nothing is sent on your behalf. The app drafts; you send.
  • No analytics SDK, no advertising, no data brokers. Nothing is fetched from LinkedIn or any platform whose terms forbid it.

What you control

  • Export everything as one JSON file, and import it back.
  • Archive or forget a person, delete a note and only what it alone supported, block a handle, choose who can read your card.
  • Delete my data wipes everything you've stored. Delete account also removes your login.

What we do not claim

We say "isolated by construction" and "granted read-only". We do not say "private", "secure" or "encrypted": data is not encrypted at rest beyond what the host provides, the operator of the server can read it, and everything sent to a model provider leaves the server.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open the app's Settings → "How Cirql works", or write to the address on the project page.
