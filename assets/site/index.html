Ask your network

Why trust it

What makes it honest

  • Every claim points back at what you said. Tap a fact or a promise and you see the exact note it came from.
  • Nothing sends. You do. Every intro, ask and reply is a draft until you tap approve.
  • Only a card you write crosses accounts, granted read-only to one specific person. Each account is isolated by construction.
